# Playable slice specification

Visual target and Tripo prerequisites precede implementation. This document fixes bounded scope before code.

## Movement and traversal

Third-person camera, WASD/arrows movement, Space jump, E nearby interaction, Q drop, R checkpoint, Escape/P pause, M mute, F fullscreen. Pointer selects nearby physical targets. Fall recovery preserves circuits and returns carried parts to a safe rack. Upper landing checkpoints. No pointer lock.

Three workshop stations: wood islands with depth/gaps, copper lift, cobalt rails, visible positive feed and negative return, yellow 6 V source and warm paper lamps. Progress requires spatial traversal.

## 1 — Wake the bridge

Carry copper to the return socket; close switch. Source → switch → 6 Ω resistive lift actuator → return → source. Jump onto powered lift, ride, jump to upper doorway. Missing return or open switch stops the lift. Optional source short trips fictional 3 A fuse.

## 2 — Split decision

Equal loads initially in series: 3 V, 0.5 A, 1.5 W each. Simplified lift travel scales with power so it cannot reach upper landing. Carry Y harness to topology socket: remove series edge and add separate feed/return edges. Parallel gives each 6 V, 1 A, 6 W. Open gate branch so spring gate opens while independent lift remains powered. Ride/jump through gate. Measurements and mechanics share the circuit solution.

## 3 — Magnet ascent

Lift and 6 Ω coil have separate branches. Restore copper coil return. Carry steel armature to upper magnetic dock for final span. Copper conducts but does not stick. Opening coil removes holding force immediately; span releases. Upper switch can hold lift while retaining coil. Recover from upper checkpoint, cross steel span, reach lamp and replay with fresh state.

## Science boundary

Tested graph and nodal solution are authority. Resistive actuators and quasi-static coil are explicit simplifications, not exact motor/inductor/battery/field simulations. Fuse trips on ideal positive-negative conductor path or strictly >3 A, latches until manual reset after unsafe connection removed. No mains.
